Help calibrate the resume benchmark

The resume scorer is tested today on invented sample resumes. Real, consented resumes would show whether its scores hold up on real applications. That is the only reason this page exists.

What happens to your resume — in plain language

  • Only the redacted version is ever stored. Names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, dates of birth, and ID numbers are stripped before anything is saved. Your original pasted text is never stored, never logged, and never shown to anyone.
  • You see exactly what will be stored first. Step 1 shows you the redacted text. It is editable — if the automatic redaction missed anything (for example a name mentioned in the middle of the text), delete it yourself before confirming.
  • Nothing is stored without your explicit consent. Until you tick the consent box and confirm, nothing is saved.
  • Reports are aggregate only. The benchmark reports averages and score distributions across all contributions. Your individual resume, your individual score, and your identity never appear in any report.
  • Delete any time. Every contribution below has a delete button. Deleting removes it permanently, so future reports cannot include it.

One honest limit: automatic name detection covers the header line of your resume and "Name:" lines. A name that appears elsewhere in the text is not auto-detected — that is why the preview is editable. Please check it before confirming.
