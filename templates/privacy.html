Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. What we collect

To provide career and research intelligence, we collect:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password — or your Google profile (name, email, avatar) when you sign in with Google.
  • Content you upload: resumes, research papers, and other documents you choose to analyze.
  • Connected profiles: GitHub and LeetCode usernames you link, plus the public profile data fetched from those services.
  • Usage data: your saved analyses, research sessions, goals, and application tracking activity within the platform.

2. How we use it

Your data is used solely to operate the platform: authenticating you, running resume and research analyses, saving your history, and improving the relevance of recommendations. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your resumes or documents for advertising.

3. Third-party services

  • Google: identity verification when you choose Google sign-in (we request only basic profile scopes).
  • Google Gemini API: document text you submit for analysis is processed by the AI model to generate results.
  • GitHub / LeetCode: public profile data is fetched only for usernames you explicitly connect.

Each of these services processes data under its own privacy policy.

4. Data storage & security

Data is stored in the application's secured database. Passwords are stored only as irreversible hashes. Uploaded files are processed and removed after analysis. We apply industry-standard safeguards, though no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your rights

You may review your saved data on your dashboard at any time. To request correction or deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us and we will action it promptly.

Self-service: download all your data anytime from Export My Data; permanently delete your account and all associated data from Delete My Account (requires password confirmation).

AI consent: before using AI features you will see a notice explaining what is sent and to whom. You can withdraw consent anytime from your profile, which disables AI features for your account.

6. Data retention

Raw uploaded files are deleted immediately after parsing. Extracted text and analyses are kept for 90 days of inactivity unless you save them. AI prompts are not stored. Access logs (without payloads) are kept 30 days. See docs/privacy/data-inventory.md for the full inventory.

7. Sub-processors

  • Render: application hosting and database.
  • Google Gemini / Groq / OpenRouter: AI processing (only text you submit to AI features).
  • GitHub / Google: sign-in and profile data you explicitly connect.

8. Grievance contact

For privacy questions or complaints: vivekbartwal76@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

10. Your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

STUDY-BUDDY is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data processed on this platform. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), you — the Data Principal — have the following rights:

  • Right to access: download everything we hold about you, anytime, from Export My Data.
  • Right to correction: update or unlink your connected GitHub and LeetCode profiles from your profile. To correct your name, email, or any other personal data, email vivekbartwal76@gmail.com and we will correct it promptly.
  • Right to erasure: permanently delete your account and all associated data from Delete My Account (requires password confirmation). Deletion hard-deletes your user record and cascade-related rows; see docs/privacy/data-inventory.md for what is covered.
  • Right to withdraw consent: AI features run only with your consent. You can withdraw it anytime from your profile, which disables AI features for your account. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done while consent was in place.
  • Right to grievance redressal: if you believe your data has been misused, or a request above has not been addressed, write to vivekbartwal76@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Children's data: the service is for users aged 13 and over. Registration requires confirming you are at least 13; if you are under 18, a parent or guardian must review this Privacy Policy and the Terms with you before you create an account.

Data breaches: if a breach exposes your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by the DPDP Act. Our internal breach-response procedure is documented in docs/privacy/breach-runbook.md.

Where your data lives: data is hosted on Render's cloud infrastructure. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not transfer it to anyone except the sub-processors listed in section 7, and only for the purposes stated there. Retention periods are in section 6.
